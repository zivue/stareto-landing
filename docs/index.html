what is being built

A calmer social layer for the things you care about.

Stareto brings personal tracking, discovery, planning, and private social features into one place without building the product around ads or attention metrics.

bucket

Track movies, books, music, games, events, and other things you want to remember, finish, rate, or revisit.

discovery

Search across indexed catalog data, open detail pages, and build a personal list from media and activities that match your interests.

planner

Turn bucket items and personal notes into dated plans, reminders, and lightweight tasks so saved things can become actual moments.

messenger

Private conversations are part of the product direction, designed for direct contact rather than public engagement loops.

profiles

Create a profile, choose what to show, follow people, and share activity without giving up control over your account data.

blog

Publish longer-form posts from your profile, with public author pages planned alongside the personal app experience.

privacy first

Your data belongs to you. Stareto is built around privacy-respecting defaults instead of tracking and data harvesting.

human connection

Built for real people and real relationships, so sharing stays focused on friends, family, and communities that matter.

no ads. no bots.

Experience authentic interactions without algorithmic manipulation, advertising surveillance, or engagement farming.

module controls

Features such as messenger, bucket, connections, planner, and blog are shaped as modules so sensitive areas can stay explicit and opt-in.
